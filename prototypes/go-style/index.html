PROTOTYPE, throwaway: gems on a Go board (round 2)

Look
Life (zoomed in; size when zoomed out)
Overlays
Drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Hover a gem for its owner, tap to pin.
Gold bezel: your gem. Pulsing gold pip: life coming. Pulsing red pip and red rim: life going. Bridge: friend. Red pinch: enemy contact. Stacked gems: an Evil collision, one gem per player. Grey cracked husk: a dead cell, which still counts.
